María Del Mar Zamora Flores

Ph.D. Candidate in Economics

Tulane University
in

I study how public programs meant for one member of a household change outcomes for everyone else in it — sibling caregivers in Mexico, working children in Bolivia, and mothers and infants years after a health transfer ends. My work sits at the intersection of development economics, the economics of education, and public policy, using administrative and survey data from Bolivia and Mexico.
